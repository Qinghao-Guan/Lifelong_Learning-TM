# Lifelong_Learning-TM
This repo has the data, codes for the paper "Lifelong Learning Research in WOS and CNKI: A Comparative Topic Modeling Analysis".

Guan, Q., Mu, W., & Liu, Z. (2026). Lifelong learning research in WOS and CNKI: A comparative topic modeling analysis. Soc. Netw. Anal. Min. *16*, Article 45. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13278-025-01562-4

Understanding and practicing lifelong learning is at the heart of community and continuing education. In the era of digitalization and artificial intelligence, how to effectively cultivate skilled talents to support the sustainable development of individuals and society has become a central challenge for educational research and practice.

This project introduces a topic modeling–based approach — combining classical methods such as LDA and BERTopic with large language models (LLMs) in the loop — to systematically map the evolution and current landscape of lifelong learning research in both CNKI (China) and Web of Science (WOS) (international). Additionally, we propose a topic utility metric to evaluate how well the identified topics align with real-world educational needs.

Our analysis reveals that Chinese research (CNKI) primarily focuses on eight topics:

- Community education and learning organization development

- Lifelong education for minority groups

- Lifelong learning and modernization

- Information technology and online education

- Learning from international experiences

- Educational platform construction

- Teacher professional development

- Credit bank construction

- Global governance and multilateral cooperation

In contrast, WOS research clusters around five major themes:

- Policy support

- Professional personnel training

- Students’ self-directed learning

- Lifelong learning for the elderly

- Teacher training and e-learning/digital literacy

Moving forward, we encourage Chinese continuing education to further integrate global best practices — such as creating a freer and more supportive academic environment, strengthening students’ self-learning abilities, and fostering reflective teaching — to promote the high-quality development of lifelong learning.
