The Chinese dataset is collected from CNKI, China’s largest academic platform. The Chinese corpus includes only titles and abstracts from CSSCI (Chinese Social Sciences Citation Index) and core journals evaluated by Peking University. Research papers from these two databases are basically selected with high standards. 

The English corpus is collected from the Web of Science, a large-scale comprehensive and multidisciplinary citation index database. All English data was collected from books, book reviews, conference proceedings, and journal articles in the Web of Science Core Collection. 

The query terms in CNKI include “终身教育” (lifelong education in English) and “终身学习” (lifelong learning in English). To align with the Chinese terms, we retrieve papers related to “lifelong education” and “lifelong learning” from the WOS database. 

One of the authors is a professional in education science thus this author filtered out the unrelated papers by hand. A total of 2174 rows of Chinese data (397,420 tokens) and 1212 rows of English data (191,884 words) were collected.
